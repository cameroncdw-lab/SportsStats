About the data. Standings, scores, team records, rosters, and league leaders load live from ESPN’s public data feed, which is compiled from official league stat sheets, and update automatically. Attendance figures come from official league / ESPN attendance reports for the season noted on each table. Ticket price figures are cited to their source (leagues do not publish official average ticket prices; the most-used industry sources are the Team Marketing Report Fan Cost Index and league gate reports). SportsStats.org is an independent reference site and is not affiliated with the NBA, WNBA, NCAA, or NFL.
